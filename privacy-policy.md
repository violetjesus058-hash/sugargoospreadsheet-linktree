# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 26, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Sugargoo Spreadsheet Link Hub** ("we," "us," or "this site") at [sugargoospreadsheet-linktree.com](https://sugargoospreadsheet-linktree.com/) handles information. This site is a lightweight quick-navigation directory for Sugargoo Spreadsheet categories, brands, guides, and external data views.

## 1. Independent navigation service

This website is not operated by Sugargoo or Linktree. The word "Linktree" is only part of the domain name; this site does not use Linktree branding, logos, colors, interface, or official-affiliation claims. We do not sell products or process third-party orders, payments, warehouse services, shipping, refunds, or after-sales support. Brand and product references belong to their respective rights holders and do not imply authorization, authenticity, quality, or endorsement.

This Policy covers this quick-link hub only. External destinations have separate privacy practices.

### How this quick-link hub works

Unlike a catalog or store, this site primarily presents a short list of routes: All Finds, a category, a brand, or the separate budget site. We may measure which route was selected, but we do not copy the destination's full product database into this hub and do not receive the destination's account, payment, warehouse, or order records.

## 2. Information collected

You can browse without creating an account. If you voluntarily contact us or use a form that becomes available, we may receive your name, email address, message, or other information you choose to provide.

Our hosting, security, and measurement systems may automatically receive your IP address, approximate IP-based location, device and operating-system details, browser type, language, referring URL, page and link interactions, navigation path, time spent, timestamps, error logs, and campaign or UTM parameters.

We or configured third-party tags may use cookies, local storage, pixels, web beacons, and similar technologies for basic operation, traffic measurement, outbound-click attribution, security, and performance analysis. We do not use this quick-link page to run an account or checkout system.

## 3. Uses of information

We use information to display and maintain quick links; understand which categories, brands, and external routes are useful; measure referral and campaign performance; analyze traffic sources; prevent automated abuse, click fraud, scraping, and attacks; diagnose failures; improve accessibility and performance; and meet legal obligations. If a configured tag supports personalized measurement or recommendations, its use is limited by that tag's settings and applicable law.

## 4. Google Tag Manager and third-party services

This site directly loads **Google Tag Manager container GTM-K5FMNTNC**. Google Tag Manager manages tags and may load measurement or attribution scripts that are actually configured in the container. We do not claim that Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any other SDK is active unless the current configuration enables it.

See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's Tag Manager information](https://support.google.com/tagmanager/answer/6102821). Hosting, security, domain, and performance providers may also process limited technical information needed to operate this hub, potentially using international infrastructure.

## 5. Sharing and third-party links

We may provide hosting, security, analytics, and referral providers with limited technical, aggregated, or pseudonymous information needed for their services. An external destination may receive a referring URL, UTM or campaign value, click time, and normal browser-request information when you follow a link. We do not sell your name, phone number, or email address through this quick-link hub.

After you leave this site or open a third-party app, that destination independently collects and uses information under its own terms. It controls accounts, payments, orders, warehousing, shipping, refunds, support, cookies, and advertising choices. Read its privacy policy before submitting data.

## 6. Storage, retention, and security

Data may be processed or stored by our providers on international infrastructure; there is no single fixed storage location that we can promise for every provider. We keep information only as long as needed for operation, referral measurement, security, legal compliance, or dispute handling, and then delete or anonymize it when practical. Provider retention settings and legal obligations may require longer retention.

Reasonable safeguards include HTTPS/TLS, access controls, limited administrator access, logging, and data minimization. No online service is risk-free. If a material incident occurs, we will investigate, contain it, and give notices required by law.

## 7. Your rights and cookie choices

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection; withdraw consent; or ask us to stop using information for personalized recommendations or referral measurement. You can disable or delete cookies and local site data in your browser, use private browsing, and adjust available advertising controls. Some link-measurement features may not work after blocking cookies.

Send a privacy request to **privacy@sugargoospreadsheet-linktree.com** with the subject "Privacy Request". We may verify your identity before responding and will respond within the period required by applicable law.

## 8. Children's privacy

This hub is intended for adults and does not knowingly solicit or intentionally collect information from children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has submitted information, contact **privacy@sugargoospreadsheet-linktree.com**. After reasonable verification, we will delete it when required and appropriate.

## 9. Updates and contact

We may revise this Policy when the site, providers, or tracking configuration changes. The current version and date will always appear on this page. Questions about privacy should be sent to **privacy@sugargoospreadsheet-linktree.com**. Questions about an external order, payment, shipment, refund, or support issue must go to the relevant destination.
